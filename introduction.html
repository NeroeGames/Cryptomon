CHAPTER I · THE EMERGENCE

A World Awakens

It began with photographs nobody could explain: a shape in the treeline, a shadow beneath the waves, a strange pair of eyes in the dark. Then came the sightings—everywhere.

Creatures once dismissed as folklore are appearing across the globe. Some resemble legends whispered for centuries. Others have no name, no known origin, and no place in any scientific record.

We call them Cryptomon.

Whether they have always lived beside us or have only recently emerged remains unknown. Their sudden visibility has transformed ordinary places into extraordinary hunting grounds.

RESTRICTED FIELD NOTES · SOCIETY ARCHIVE

The Age of Discovery

Explorers, amateur photographers, and researchers race to document the unknown. Yet observation is only the beginning: a remarkable new device can contain a Cryptomon within an instant photograph.

Across the world, people have begun collecting these living mysteries and summoning them for battles. Friendly matches have become organized competitions, while rumors circulate about dangerous, altered creatures.

What We Know

Each recorded Cryptomon exhibits an elemental affinity and distinctive abilities. Some can change into more powerful Mythic forms; darker Feral transformations remain poorly understood.

This volume is a field guide, not a final answer. Every new sighting may rewrite what we believe.
