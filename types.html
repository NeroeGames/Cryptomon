The Six Affinities

Every Cryptomon has one known affinity. A creature's type shapes its instincts, habitat, and the techniques it can wield in battle.

🌿 Plant — roots, spores, growth and living terrain.

💧 Water — tides, currents, rain and hidden depths.

🔥 Fire — flame, heat, embers and combustion.

🌪 Air — wind, flight, pressure and swift movement.

⚡ Lightning — electrical surges, charged energy and speed.

◈ Normal — physical prowess, instinct and unusual neutral techniques.

Affinity alone does not decide a battle. Timing, environment, and the bond between photographer and Cryptomon matter just as much.
