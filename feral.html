01 / Nature of the anomaly

When evolution goes wrong

A Cryptomon's Mythic form is a more powerful expression of its nature. A Feral form is something else: a corrupted alternate evolution in which familiar traits become distorted, abilities grow unstable, and behavior can turn dangerously unpredictable.

The transformation does not simply make a creature stronger. A guardian may turn against its own forest; a harmless light may lure travelers into a marsh. In many records, the very gift that once defined a creature becomes its greatest danger.

Feral forms are not a separate elemental affinity, nor are they a stage beyond Mythic. They represent an alternative path within the same Cryptomon family. What determines which path a creature takes remains unknown.

03 / Preliminary observations

Recognizing the change

01

Distorted morphology

Exaggerated claws, altered silhouettes and crimson eyes recur in many specimens. Appearance alone is not proof of corruption; some ordinary Cryptomon naturally have red eyes.

02

Unstable affinity

Familiar abilities become difficult to contain. Plants overgrow, water surges, flames spread and electrical disturbances may outlast the creature's presence.

03

Behavioral divergence

Previously gentle or protective species may display hostility, disorientation or destructive behavior. The degree of awareness retained by a Feral creature is unknown.

05 / Unresolved research

What causes a Feral evolution?

The Society has not established a mechanism. The following are working hypotheses, not confirmed findings.

Hypothesis A

Affinity overload

Perhaps elemental power accumulates faster than a Cryptomon can regulate it, causing its natural abilities to distort. This could explain unstable powers, but not why some creatures transform while others do not.

Hypothesis B

Instinct under pressure

Fear, isolation or environmental stress might push evolution toward an extreme defensive form. The theory remains untested; stress alone has not been established as a trigger.

Hypothesis C

An external influence

Something not yet understood may interfere with the living signature of a Cryptomon. Whether that influence is environmental, another creature, or a deeper phenomenon remains speculation.

Unanswered questions

Can a Feral transformation be reversed? Does a creature remember its former bonds? Can photographic containment remain stable during a transformation? No reliable answers are recorded in this volume.

06 / Society field advisory

Observe. Document. Withdraw.

In suspected Feral encounters, prioritize the safety of the field team and nearby Cryptomon. Do not provoke a specimen to obtain a clearer photograph. Record its habitat, behavior and affinity from a safe distance, and report anomalies in the Crypto-Camera to the Society before attempting another capture.

Until the phenomenon is understood, no Feral creature should be assumed beyond recovery.
